# HALF-A-BUCK

## 8-Bit Birthday Stealth Game

**Project MV Doc (Minimum Viable Design)**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Core Concept

**Premise:**\
On the way to Brooklyn to celebrate your 50th birthday, you hop into an
Uber.\
Suddenly --- knockout gas. Black screen.

You wake up inside a mysterious compound.

Your mission: **Escape.**

Twist ending: After navigating the compound and escaping, you discover
it was an elaborate birthday surprise orchestrated by family and
friends.

Tone:\
Retro stealth thriller with subtle humor and a heartfelt ending.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Platform & Tech Stack

**Target Platform:**\
Browser-based (HTML5)

**Hosting:**\
GitHub Pages (via GabeVelez.com repo)

**Engine/Framework:**\
Phaser.js (lightweight 2D HTML game framework)

**Art Style:**\
8-bit / 16-bit top-down pixel aesthetic

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Game Structure

### Opening Cinematic (Retro Still Frames)

Use static 8-bit pixel scenes with scrolling text (Metal Gear style).

Scene 1:\
You entering an Uber.\
Text scrolls: \> "Brooklyn. 50 years. Half a Buck."

Scene 2:\
Inside Uber --- suspicious driver.\
Text scrolls: \> "Something feels... off."

Scene 3:\
Green gas fills screen.\
Fade to black.\
Text: \> "You have been compromised."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Gameplay Style

**Perspective:**\
Top-down stealth view

**Core Mechanics:**

-   Sneak past guards
-   Non-lethal knockouts only
-   Hide unconscious guards in closets/rooms
-   Avoid detection cones
-   Collect small key items (keycards, codes)
-   Simple environmental puzzles

No killing. Pure stealth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Level Breakdown (MVP Version)

### Level 1: The Cell

-   Wake up in locked room
-   Learn movement controls
-   Find hidden key
-   Avoid first patrol guard

### Level 2: Hallway Sector

-   Guard patrol patterns
-   Introduce knockout mechanic
-   Introduce hiding bodies
-   Security camera avoidance

### Level 3: Storage Area

-   Crates and shadows for hiding
-   Retrieve access card
-   Slight difficulty increase

### Final Level: Exit Zone

-   Alarm fake-out moment
-   Dramatic music
-   Escape through final door

Fade to black.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Final Twist Scene

Retro still frame.

Lights turn on.

Family appears in pixel form.

Text scrolls:

> "Happy 50th Birthday." "Mission Complete, Half-A-Buck."

End screen:\
Pixel cake.\
Score summary.\
Optional chiptune Happy Birthday theme.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. MVP Scope (Keep It Small)

Minimum version should include:

-   3 playable levels
-   1 knockout mechanic
-   1 hide-body mechanic
-   1 intro cinematic sequence
-   1 ending twist scene
-   Basic sound effects
-   Simple pixel art (minimal animation)

Target total playtime: 10--15 minutes

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Art Direction Notes

Character sprite: - Simple pixel Gabe - Slightly exaggerated head - Dark
clothing for stealth

Guards: - Generic silhouettes - Flashlight or vision cone indicator

Environment: - Dark floors - Industrial walls - Shadow corners

Keep asset count minimal.
