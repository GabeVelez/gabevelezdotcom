# HALF-A-BUCK

## Mission: Brooklyn

### 16-Bit Tactical Stealth Birthday Game

**Updated MV Design Document**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. CORE CONCEPT

On the night of your 50th birthday, you leave your Whitestone apartment
heading to Brooklyn to celebrate.

You never arrive.

Intercepted by the fictional villain organization known as the **HATE
Brigade**, you awaken inside a mysterious compound.

Your mission: Escape. Reach Brooklyn. Celebrate.

Tone: Retro 16-bit stealth thriller with cinematic tension and a
triumphant birthday twist.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. GAMEPLAY SYSTEMS

Perspective: Top-down stealth

Core Mechanics: - Movement - Crouch - Non-lethal knockout - Drag
bodies - Hide bodies - Cardboard box concealment

Enemy Types: 1. Guards -- simple patrols 2. Lead Guards -- larger patrol
loops 3. Overseer Soldiers -- long vision cones, harder detection zones

No killing. Pure stealth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. BOARD STRUCTURE

## Board 1 --- Warehouse

Environment: - Crates - Industrial walkways - Cameras

Introduces: - Basic stealth mechanics - First guard types - Cardboard
box mechanic

Clue discovered: "Operation HALF-BUCK"

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Board 2 --- Wine Cellar Maze

Environment: - Stone corridors - Barrels - Low lighting

New Mechanic: Molotov distraction tool (environmental use only)

Used to: - Block patrol paths temporarily - Burn rope barriers - Create
light distractions

Difficulty increases.

Flight manifest discovered.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Board 3 --- Cigar Humidor

Environment: - Warm wood tones - Humid mist - Gold trim

New Mechanic: Butane lighter micro-flame

Used to: - Clear wooden obstacles - Activate heat triggers - Create
timed barriers

Hardest stealth level.

Exit leads to runway.
