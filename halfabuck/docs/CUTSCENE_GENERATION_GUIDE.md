- Passenger is in BACK SEAT
- Driver is in FRONT SEAT (ahead of passenger)
- Glass PARTITION is BETWEEN them (vertical barrier)
- Passenger is facing FORWARD toward the partition/driver
- Hand pressed against partition glass (NOT rear window or side window)
- Driver visible THROUGH the partition glass in FRONT of passenger

- Hispanic man in his 40s with beard and glasses
- Black baseball cap with white "G" logo (still on head)
- Black suit visible

- Green and yellow toxic gas filling the BACK COMPARTMENT (behind partition)

- Hand contact point clearly on partition glass

- Harsh, danger lighting
- Sickly green/yellow glow from gas in back area
- High contrast
- Dramatic shadows

## Troubleshooting

### If Character Doesn't Match Reference Photos
- Re-upload reference photos to ChatGPT
- Emphasize "use reference photos for likeness" in prompt
- Try: "Make the character look exactly like the reference photos provided"

### If Style is Too Realistic or Too Cartoony
- Emphasize "illustrated 16-bit video game cutscene style"
- Reference specific games: "like Metal Gear Solid PS1 cutscenes"
- Add "painterly aesthetic" or "not photorealistic, not cartoon"

### If Aspect Ratio is Wrong
- Specify "16:9 aspect ratio, 1280x720 resolution or similar"
- Regenerate and request correct dimensions

### If Colors are Off
- Be more specific about color palette in prompt
- Add color hex codes if needed
- Request "warm sunset tones" or "heavy red color grading"
