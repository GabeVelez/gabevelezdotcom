Keep it empty
Scenery on the deck reads as cover and there is none — his rush goes through the space you would hide in. Vents and aircon units belong at the very top and bottom edges as dressing, not in the middle where they would break the chase.
Why he lands mid-roof
Far enough right that he is between you and the pad, far enough left that you are not immediately in his reach. About 100px of clear ground either side on landing.
The weapon is short of the pad
Dropped just left of the helipad, so collecting it means going to the far end — past him — rather than finding it on the way.
Collision: a wall band around all four edges, and nothing else. The helipad is a painted circle, not an obstacle. Author it in Figma at 768×180 like the others, rectangles only — a path exports as a shape the parser drops silently.
