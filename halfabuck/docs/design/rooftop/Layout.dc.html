The roof

768 × 256, and the screen is 384 × 180

Two screens wide and half again as tall. It was one screen tall, which made the deck a 100px strip a lunge crossed in a single move — there was nowhere to step to. The deck is 172 deep now and a lunge covers half of it.

The drop goes at the top, and that is not a preference
Every finished background draws the far wall face-on and leaves the near edge as plain floor — the executive panelling and sconces, the sewer's lit wall and ladder, the cell's barred window, all across the top; all three bottom edges are floor. The camera is tilted looking up-screen.
The interiors cannot say what is past the near edge, because in a room nothing is. You see over both edges, but a sight line toward the top is shallow and runs out across the whole city, while one toward the bottom is nearly vertical and catches only a compressed sliver of our own facade and the street beneath. 58px against 24. That lopsidedness is the tilt; make the two match and the camera flattens back out.

Keep it empty
One air-conditioning unit against the far parapet, and nothing else. It is the only thing a rush can slam into, which is worth having; a second one nearer the pad was taking room out of the half of the roof the chase actually happens in.
The roof got taller, not thinner
Making the sky and the drop legible cost depth, and the answer was not to shrink them back — it was to stop assuming the level had to be as tall as the screen. Nothing else in the game does. The deck is 160 deep now, 60% more than it was, with the view intact.
You come out at mid-height
Through a door in the left wall rather than a stairwell box on the deck: it costs no floor, and it starts you in the middle of the lane instead of pinned against the top of it. The weapon drops short of the pad, so collecting it still means going the whole way.
Collision: a wall band around all four edges plus the one unit — five rectangles, and that is the whole level. The helipad is paint, not an obstacle. Rectangles only: a path exports as a shape the parser drops silently, which is how the executive wing nearly shipped with no top wall.
