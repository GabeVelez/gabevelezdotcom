Keep it empty
One air-conditioning unit against the far parapet, and nothing else. It is the only thing a rush can slam into, which is worth having; a second one nearer the pad was taking room out of the half of the roof the chase actually happens in.
Why he lands mid-roof
Far enough right that he is between you and the pad, far enough left that you are not immediately in his reach. About 100px of clear ground either side on landing.
You come out at mid-height
Through a door in the left wall rather than a stairwell box on the deck: it costs no floor, and it starts you in the middle of the lane instead of pinned against the top of it. The weapon drops short of the pad, so collecting it still means going the whole way.
Collision: a wall band around all four edges plus the one unit — five rectangles, and that is the whole level. The helipad is paint, not an obstacle. Rectangles only: a path exports as a shape the parser drops silently, which is how the executive wing nearly shipped with no top wall.
