Frames to generate

Eight frames close the game

All at 1536 × 640, the same 2.4:1 as the abduction set, prompt and style block in docs/CUTSCENE_PROMPT.md. Generate each group in one pass — frame by frame is where the character stops looking like the same character.

Drop them in assets/cutscenes/story/ under these names and they play. No code changes; the two groups are already wired and currently sit empty.

THE SHOT cs_shot_01 – 02  ·  new group
cs_shot_01
Low and behind you, launcher on the shoulder, the monster filling the far end of the roof. Backblast lighting the deck. He is mid-rush toward camera — you are firing at something already coming.
cs_shot_02
Impact. White core, the silhouette of him inside it. No gore — this is a birthday game.
THE ESCAPE cs_escape_01 – 03  ·  already wired, empty
cs_escape_01
The roof going up behind you as you run for the open door. Rotor wash, debris, orange from below.
cs_escape_02
Wide, from the air: the helicopter banking away, the roof burning behind it, the skyline beyond. The only frame with any distance in it.
cs_escape_03
Inside the cabin, side on, sitting in the passenger seat. Cigar in your mouth, city sliding past the window behind you. Warm light from the instruments. This is the last thing anyone sees.
Passenger, not pilot — which is what makes the side view the right one. A cockpit shot would have you flying it, and would put the back of your head to the camera at the exact moment your face is the point.
THE TRANSFORMATION cs_transform_01 – 03  ·  already wired, empty
cs_transform_01–03
The empty office he was left in, then the change, then whatever is standing there at the end of it. Three beats, no more. Nobody is watching this but the audience — frame it as something happening in a room with no one in it.
Settled — it plays on the stairs
Not when the water lands, and not when he touches down on the roof. It runs after the scripted escape, once the character is out of the room. He never sees it; you do. That gap is the whole point — his walk across the roof in act one is worth watching precisely because he does not know.
One thing to watch: the abduction set came back with faces cropped because the frames were 1.5:1 and the window is 2.4:1. Ask for the wide ratio explicitly and keep everything that matters in the central band — at this shape the top and bottom go first.
