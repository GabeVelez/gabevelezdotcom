# Cutscene Sound Effects Needed

Place the following sound files in this directory: `halfabuck/public/assets/sounds/cutscenes/`

## Required Sound Files:

### 1. door_lock.mp3
**Frame 5 - The Button**
- Sound: Heavy door locks engaging (metallic CLUNK/CHUNK sound)
- Duration: ~1-2 seconds
- Example: Car central locking sound, prison cell door locking
- Should sound ominous and final

### 2. partition_raise.mp3
**Frames 6 & 7 - Partition Open/Sealed**
- Sound: Glass/mechanical partition sliding up and sealing
- Duration: ~2-3 seconds (plays across both frames)
- Example: Mechanical whirr, glass sliding, hydraulic seal
- Should sound smooth but menacing

### 3. gas_cough.mp3
**Frame 9 - Gas Attack**
- Sound: Gas hissing + coughing/choking
- Duration: ~1.5-2 seconds
- Example: Gas leak hiss + person coughing desperately
- Layered sound: hissing in background, coughing in foreground

## Sound File Formats:
- **Format:** MP3 (preferred) or OGG
- **Sample Rate:** 44.1kHz recommended
- **Bit Rate:** 128kbps or higher
- **Channels:** Mono or Stereo

## Where to Find/Create Sounds:

### Free Sound Resources:
- **Freesound.org** - High quality, free sound effects
- **OpenGameArt.org** - Game-specific sounds
- **Zapsplat.com** - Free for personal/commercial use
- **SoundBible.com** - Public domain sounds

### AI Sound Generation:
- **ElevenLabs Sound Effects** - Generate custom sounds from text
- **AudioCraft by Meta** - Open source sound generation

## Testing:
After adding sound files, refresh the browser and play the cutscene. Sound effects should play at:
- Frame 5: Door lock sound
- Frame 6-7: Partition raising sound
- Frame 9: Gas hissing and coughing

Volume can be adjusted in the game's sound settings.
